Real-Time Workforce Dashboard

Shift Desk

Watches a full team of call center employees at once — ready, on a call, after-call work, break, or administrative task — automatically, and rolls it all up into live stats plus daily, weekly, and monthly team reports.

Each agent works through their own shift automatically — calls, wrap-up, breaks, and admin tasks — on their own timing. This view just observes and updates live; nobody here is driving it.

Backfills realistic shift history for the past 13 days across all 5 team members, so the weekly and monthly reports have real data to show. Today stays live — this doesn't touch today's data.
